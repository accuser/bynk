# 0348 — `EventSubscriberShape` reads `IrItem::Service` instead of walking the raw AST — the first real, shipped call site for the dormant `lower_service_item_ir`

- **Status:** Accepted (v0.249)

summary: Completes P6.23 now that its own prerequisite safety gap is closed (#1251-#1254) — the architecture was already sound, and the body-lowering path it depends on is now proven safe on every real corpus body

**Context.** `EventSubscriberShape` (`#1232`/`#1233`) captures two booleans per event-subscriber service — `two_param_handler` (does the `on event` handler take the two-arg envelope form) and `schema_dispatch` (does the service declare a `via schema(...)` filter) — at each unit's own check time, since its `CheckedProgram` doesn't survive past `check_unit_files`'s per-file loop. Until now it read these directly off the service's own raw AST (`s.handlers`/`s.protocol`), even though `IrItem::Service`/`lower_service_item_ir` (P6.11, #1171) already carry the identical data in a real, already-lowered form — they simply had zero shipped call sites anywhere, the same gap this track's own §6 "Sixteenth" entry named as "the *second* time in this track a fresh accumulator got built to answer a cross-unit question `IrItem::Service` already carries the data for."

The reason it wasn't converted at the time: `lower_service_item_ir` lowers every handler's own *body*, not just its declared shape, and nothing had verified that was safe to do on real production service bodies. #1244's own safety investigation found it wasn't — `lower_service_item_ir` panicked on ~51 of the corpus's real service bodies, across 20 distinct services, five root causes. #1251 through #1254 closed every one of them.

**Decision.** Replace the raw `s.handlers`/`s.protocol` walk with a call to `lower_service_item_ir(s, &program)`, reading `two_param_handler`/`schema_dispatch` off the resulting `IrItem::Service`'s own `handlers`/`protocol: ProtocolIr::Events { schema_dispatch, .. }` instead. The cheap `matches!(&s.protocol, ServiceProtocol::Events { .. })` pre-filter stays in front of the lowering call — this is a structural question (*which* services even have a shape worth capturing), not a resurrected raw-AST *read* of the data itself, and it avoids paying for a full body-lowering pass on every HTTP/Cron/Queue/WebSocket service in the project just to discard the result.

**Consequences.** This is the first real, shipped emitter call site for `IrItem::Service`/`lower_service_item_ir` — dormant since P6.11 landed. Verified by a full zero-diff bless against the entire `bynkc` e2e fixture corpus, including `1232_events_envelope_schema_dispatch_bare` (the fixture that specifically pins the `schema_dispatch` half of this predicate, confirmed to fail if that disjunct regresses) — byte-identical generated output confirms the IR-driven read reproduces the raw-AST walk's own answer for every real service in the corpus, not just the ones this PR's own review happened to check. `ast_importers` unaffected (`project.rs` was already counted).
